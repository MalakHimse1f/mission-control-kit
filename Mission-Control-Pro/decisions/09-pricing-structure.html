Pricing shape determines what the marketing site even shows.
What's the model?

The problem Buyer is the eng manager or CTO. Value scales with team size and depth of org adoption. We need a model that's easy to predict on a budget, hard to game, and sticky once a team has built up workflows.
Decided 2026-05-26 · Path A

Per-seat with Free / Team / Enterprise tiers. Free is solo-user only — single user in the org, no teammates, no cloud-shared workflows. Team tier ~$30/seat/mo; any account in an org counts as a seat (PMs, designers, and engineers all count). Enterprise is custom. Stripe Customer Portal manages plans + seats.

How does Mission Control Pro charge?

A Decided
Per-seat with Free / Team / Enterprise
Free single-user. Team at ~$30/seat/month. Enterprise custom. Any account in an org = a seat (PMs/designers count). Annual discount.
Tier ladder
Free — 1 user, local-only
Team — $30/seat/mo
Enterprise — custom, SSO, self-host
Pros
  • Predictable budget for CTOs
  • Stripe-friendly
  • Free tier feeds Team via team growth
Cons
  • Per-seat can feel high for non-engineering members
  • Need anti-share-account enforcement
B Not chosen
Usage-based — per agent session or per decision
Charge per metered unit: number of agent sessions per month, or per resolved decision. Free quota included.

Pros

Cons

  • Hard to forecast revenue early

Hybrid — per-seat base + usage overage
Per-seat covers a quota of sessions/decisions. Overage billed metered. Best for power users, but two bills to explain.
